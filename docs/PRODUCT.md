# Fork & Flex product brief

## Promise

Fork & Flex gives a user the next realistic workout, food, or recovery decision
when the day no longer matches the original plan.

## Initial audience

Busy beginners who want to lose fat while maintaining strength, but struggle
with rigid plans and all-or-nothing thinking.

## Differentiator

The first release will focus on context-aware recovery and Minimum Viable Day
planning. It will not attempt to become another exhaustive calorie tracker.

## First user journey

1. Set a goal and practical constraints.
2. Receive a simple baseline day plan.
3. Tap “My day changed.”
4. Describe what happened using quick choices and optional text.
5. Receive one recommended next move, an easier alternative, and a short reason.
6. Mark what was actually completed so future suggestions improve.

## Safety boundaries

- Do not recommend compensatory fasting, purging, dehydration, or exercise.
- Do not provide injury diagnosis or medical nutrition therapy.
- Do not set targets below safe configurable limits.
- Screen for high-risk language and provide supportive escalation guidance.
- Explain uncertainty in AI-estimated meals and let users correct all estimates.

## First milestone

Build the onboarding, daily check-in, and deterministic Minimum Viable Day
generator with sample data before connecting a model or collecting real health
information.
